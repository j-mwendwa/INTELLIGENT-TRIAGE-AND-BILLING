# Billing Policies & Procedures

## Invoice & Billing Cycle

All customers are billed on a monthly cycle starting from their subscription date. Invoices are generated automatically on the 1st of each month and sent to the registered billing email address.

### Invoice Contents
Each invoice includes:
- Subscription plan charges (base fee)
- Usage-based charges (API calls above plan limit)
- Any applicable taxes (VAT/GST based on customer location)
- Credits applied from previous billing disputes or promotions
- Net amount due

### Payment Terms
Payment is due within **14 days** of invoice date. Late payments incur a 1.5% monthly fee after the grace period.

---

## Refund Policy

### Eligibility
Refunds are available in the following situations:
1. **Billing error**: Charges that were applied in error (e.g., system bug, double-charge)
2. **Service downtime**: Pro-rated credit for verified outages exceeding SLA thresholds (99.9% monthly uptime)
3. **Cancellation within 30 days**: New customers who cancel within 30 days of signup receive a full refund

### Non-Refundable Items
- Usage-based charges already consumed
- Overage charges for API calls made
- Setup fees and professional services

### Refund Process
1. Submit a refund request via the billing portal or support ticket
2. Include your invoice number and reason for refund
3. Refunds are reviewed within **3 business days**
4. Approved refunds are processed within **5-7 business days** to the original payment method

## Payment Methods

Accepted payment methods:
- **Credit/debit cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- **Bank transfer (ACH)**: US accounts only, 3-5 day processing
- **Wire transfer**: International, $25 processing fee applies
- **Wise**: Available for plans under $500/month

### Failed Payments
If a payment fails:
1. We retry automatically after 3 days
2. Account is suspended after 2 failed retries
3. A $15 reactivation fee applies after suspension
4. Accounts suspended for more than 60 days may be terminated

---

## Account Credits

Credits are applied automatically to the next invoice. Credits do not expire and are non-transferable.

### How to Earn Credits
- Referral program: $50 credit per referred customer who stays 3+ months
- Service credit requests: Approved outage claims
- Promotional codes: Apply in billing settings

---

## Billing Disputes

To dispute a charge:
1. Log into your account and go to **Billing > Dispute a Charge**
2. Select the invoice and specific line item
3. Provide a reason and any supporting documentation
4. Our billing team responds within **2 business days**

Disputed amounts are held in escrow during review — your service remains active.
